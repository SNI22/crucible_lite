## Per-gripper TCP / contact-point offset — method locked

The tilt test pivots about the **contact point**, which is at a
gripper-specific z-offset below `fr3_hand_tcp` (the FR3's standard 45 mm
fingertip plane). Two paths considered:

- **CAD measurement** — mount-to-tip distance `L` from the finger's
  screw-hole mating plane (matches URDF's 58.4 mm carriage reference).
  Caveats: datum slop between the screw-hole face and the URDF reference,
  plus the screw axis is perpendicular to the mating face (Franka fingers
  bolt onto a vertical face → screws horizontal). Robust form: measure
  the **z-component (axial) drop** from the screw-hole center to the tip.
- **Differential touch-off** (locked path) — probe the same (x, y)
  table point with each gripper using `probe_surface.py`. The reported
  `fr3_hand_tcp` z at contact differs across grippers by exactly the
  tip-extension offset: `Δz = z_finray − z_jaw`. **Datum-free.** No
  reference plane assumed; resolves the URDF/CAD ambiguity in hardware.

Action item once on hardware: run `probe_surface.py` (5 taps, 2 N) at
one common (x, y) with each of the four grippers (G1–G4) and report the
four `z_ground_truth.json` files. The Δz values become the per-gripper
TCP / tilt-pivot offsets.

## Symmetry argument → halved tilt sweep

Each gripper is mirror-symmetric across the plane perpendicular to its
**gripping direction** (the two fingers/blades are identical). Combined
with a straight cloth edge (mirror-symmetric about its perpendicular),
this gives `+θ ⇔ −θ` for the two tilt axes that lie *in* the mirror
plane. Only rotation about the **gripping direction itself** breaks the
symmetry (both fingers tilt together; one direction noses into the
cloth body, the other off the edge).

User-noted convention: the gripping direction is the **y-axis** in
their EEF frame → the mirror plane is **xz**, and rotation about **y**
is the asymmetric axis. Pending one final confirmation (Open Item 1 in
experiment.md). Recommendation: empirically validate the mirror
assumption on G1 (full ±5° on the two "symmetric" axes) before
committing to half-sweeps on G2–G4.

## Experiment matrix (locked in `docs/experiment.md`)

- **4 grippers**: G1 parallel jaw (rigid), G2 parallel jaw + TPU 90 A
  pad, G3 finray 33 mm, G4 finray 18 mm.
- **Pilot** (per gripper): touch-off (`probe_surface.py`),
  max-depth escalation, success-threshold scan. ≈ 35 trials.
- **Study 1** (depth sweep, tilt = 0):
  - G1, G2: −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 mm × 3 reps = 21 each
  - G3, G4: −1, 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 mm × 3 reps = 18 each
  - Total: 78 trials.
- **Study 2** (tilt × depth, two depths per gripper):
  - Levels: 0, 2.5, 5° on each of x, y, z (one axis at a time);
    14 conditions × 3 reps × 4 grippers = 168 trials.
  - Depths: `d_min` (= `d_min_success`) and
    `d_safe` (= mean of `d_min_success` and `max_depth_safe`).
- **Grand total**: ≈ 280 trials (3–4 sessions).
