MTS (Mechanical Testing System) machine is available to the user for A301 FlexiForce sensor calibration. Provides controlled, precise force generation across the sensor's range.

**Why:** A301 FlexiForce sensors are nonlinear and unit-to-unit variable; calibration against a traceable force source is required before per-foot readings are trustworthy. MTS gives that traceable source without needing to procure or rig a separate load setup.

**How to apply:** When calibration of the A301-1 or A301-25 sensors comes up — in Bills, advisor suggestions, or signal-model design — assume MTS-based calibration is the planned method. Don't suggest alternative force-generation rigs (hanging weights, hand-press) unless the user signals MTS is unavailable.
